Frequently asked questions

What file formats will I receive?

Most logo designers provide PNG, JPG, and PDF formats in the basic package. Premium packages include vector files (AI, EPS, SVG) which can be scaled to any size without losing quality.

How long does it take to get a logo?

Delivery times vary by seller and package. Express options can deliver in 24 hours, while standard packages typically take 2-5 days. Custom projects may take longer depending on complexity.

Can I request revisions?

Yes! Most packages include multiple revisions. Standard and Premium packages often include unlimited revisions to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final design.

Will I own the copyright to my logo?

Yes, once the project is completed and paid for, you receive full commercial rights and copyright to your logo. You can use it however you wish for your business.

What information do I need to provide?

You should provide your company name, industry, target audience, preferred colors, style preferences, and any specific ideas or inspiration. The more details you provide, the better the designer can meet your needs.
