Frequently asked questions

What type of content will I receive?

You will receive well-researched, plagiarism-free content tailored to your requirements. Formats include DOCX, PDF, and TXT, ready for blogs, websites, or social media.

How long does it take to deliver the content?

Delivery times vary by seller and package. Express options can deliver in 24 hours, while standard packages typically take 2-5 days. Custom projects may take longer depending on complexity.

Can I request revisions?

Yes! Most packages include multiple revisions. Standard and Premium packages often include unlimited revisions to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final design.

Will I own the copyright to my Content?

Yes, once the project is completed and paid for, you receive full commercial rights and copyright to your content. You can use it however you wish for your business.

What information do I need to provide?

Provide your topic, keywords, target audience, word count, writing style preferences, and any reference material. The more details you provide, the better the content matches your requirements.
