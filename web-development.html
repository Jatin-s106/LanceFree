Frequently asked questions

What technologies do you use for web development?

We use modern technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, PHP, and WordPress depending on your project needs. You can request a specific tech stack as well.

How long does it take to build a website?

Delivery time depends on the package and project complexity. Basic websites take 2–5 days, Standard packages may take 5–7 days, and Premium websites may require 7–14 days for advanced features.

Can I request revisions?

Yes! Most packages include multiple revisions. Standard and Premium packages often include unlimited revisions to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final design.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely. All websites we build are fully responsive and work perfectly on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

Do you provide hosting or domain?

Hosting and domain are not included, but we can guide you to the best providers and even help set them up if needed.

Do you provide hosting or domain?

We will need your business name, website purpose, preferred style, features you want, content (text/images), and reference websites if available. More details help us build exactly what you want.
