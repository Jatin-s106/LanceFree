Frequently asked questions

What will I receive after app development?

You will receive the complete mobile application source code, compiled app files (APK/IPA if applicable), assets, and documentation. Premium packages also include backend and deployment support.

How long does it take to develop a mobile app?

Delivery times vary by seller and package. Express options can deliver in 24 hours, while standard packages typically take 2-5 days. Custom projects may take longer depending on complexity.

Can I request revisions or changes?

Yes! Most packages include multiple revisions. Standard and Premium packages often include unlimited revisions to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final design.

Will I own the source code and app?

Yes, once the project is completed and payment is made, you receive full ownership of the source code and commercial rights to use the application.

What information do I need to provide?

You should share your app idea, target platform (Android/iOS), required features, reference apps (if any), and design preferences. Clear requirements help ensure faster and better development.
